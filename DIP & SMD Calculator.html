Reference: EIA-96 table and multipliers

Multiplier letters: Z ×0.001 · Y/R ×0.01 · X/S ×0.1 · A ×1 · B/H ×10 · C ×100 · D ×1000 · E ×10000 · F ×100000. Marking = two-digit code + letter (e.g. 01B = 100 × 10 = 1 kΩ).

Reference: how each marking is built

3-digit: two significant digits + multiplier digit (0–6), e.g. 472 = 47 × 10² = 4.7 kΩ. Values below 10 Ω cannot be written this way.

4-digit: three significant digits + multiplier digit (1002 = 100 × 10² = 10 kΩ). Below 100 Ω, R marks the decimal point (10R0, 4R70).

R-notation: R is the decimal point, up to four characters (R100 = 0.1 Ω, 2R2 = 2.2 Ω, 22R0 = 22 Ω).

E-series are preferred values (E6/E12/E24: two significant digits; E48/E96/E192: three). They say which values are manufactured, not how a part is marked.
